# Design-Partner Pilot Plan

## Offer

> We have built an early working prototype for recording anchor inspections and preparing draft reports. It is not a finished compliance product. We are looking for one or two inspection companies to help us correct the workflow. In exchange, we will adapt the general product to the partner’s workflow and provide free access during the pilot.

## Partner provides

- Access to a small number of staff for workflow interviews, demonstrations and
  supervised field feedback.
- Anonymised or appropriately permissioned sample registers, photographs,
  checklists and reports.
- Device, connectivity and export/import examples.
- Timely feedback and a named operational contact.

## Project provides

- A bounded anchor-inspection workflow, mobile field capture, office review and
  prototype draft reports.
- Onboarding, reasonable support during agreed pilot hours, and workflow fixes
  that improve the general product.
- Clear handling of known limitations and no claim of certification or
  automatic compliance.

## Boundaries and expectations

Partner data is used only for agreed pilot purposes, access is limited, and
private files are not public by default. The general product, reusable code and
generic workflow improvements remain with the project. Partner-specific report
templates, wording, branding and operational material remain subject to
agreement; proposed terms require legal review. No legal claims are made by
this plan.

Feedback should be weekly during setup and field use, with a short review after
each pilot batch. Pilot scope is Queensland, installed single-point roof
anchors, one primary inspector per job, limited offline operation and draft
reports. No final compliance certificate, customer portal or broad asset
catalogue is promised.

## Success criteria

- A partner can prepare and assign a representative job.
- An inspector can complete a representative visit with the agreed offline
  duration and no silent evidence loss.
- Photos and anchor outcomes are associated correctly after sync.
- A reviewer can find missing information and produce a useful draft report.
- Partner feedback identifies a credible repeatable workflow and the next
  product decisions.

## Exit conditions

Exit, pause or re-scope if data permissions are unclear, the workflow cannot be
used safely with human review, sync loses evidence, or the partner cannot
provide the agreed feedback. Pilot completion does not imply certification,
compliance validation or production readiness.
