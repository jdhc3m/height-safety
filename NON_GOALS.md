# MVP Non-goals and Future Scope

The following are not immediate implementation scope and must not appear as
implicit commitments in active requirements:

- Lifelines, static lines, guardrails, walkways, ladders, rope-access systems,
  roof-access systems, or other asset families.
- Automatic compliance, certification, standards interpretation, inspection
  interval decisions, universal manufacturer logic, or jurisdiction rule engines.
- Customer-configurable compliance rules, complex CRM, accounting, payroll,
  invoicing, advanced scheduling, customer portal, public verification,
  AI-generated technical conclusions, or enterprise integrations.
- Fully offline final approval or concurrent multi-inspector offline editing.
- Microservices, event streaming, Kubernetes, or a generic rules engine unless
  the future codebase genuinely requires them.

These may be reconsidered only after design-partner evidence and a recorded
decision. Broad historical requirements are preserved in
`docs/archive/stage-2a-broad-research/`.
