# Product Vision

## Product definition

> An early-stage mobile and web application that helps height-safety inspectors prepare anchor inspection jobs, identify installed anchors, record observations and photographs in the field, review collected evidence and generate draft inspection reports.

The product supports the inspector’s workflow. It does not replace the
authorised inspector, reviewer, engineer or certifier, and it does not make
professional, legal, engineering, standards or compliance decisions.

## Initial target

Small Australian height-safety inspection companies, beginning in Queensland,
using one design-partner pilot. The first asset is an installed single-point
roof anchor. The first release is a workflow prototype, not a finished
compliance or certification platform.

## Product promise

Reduce avoidable field and office administration by keeping the anchor register,
field evidence, review questions and draft report together. The system records
what the inspector entered and what the reviewer did; it does not claim that
the recorded outcome is legally or technically validated.

## Language rules

Use: draft inspection report, recorded inspection outcome, submitted by
inspector, reviewed by, report prepared by, and further assessment required.

Do not use: certified by, automatically compliant, guaranteed compliant,
engineer-grade certificate, or fully standards-compliant.
