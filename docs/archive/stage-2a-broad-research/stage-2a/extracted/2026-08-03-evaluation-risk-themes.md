# Extracted Risk Themes — Technical-Lead Evaluation

> Historical reference. This document does not define the current MVP scope.

## Status

These are unverified risk hypotheses extracted from the submitted evaluation. They are intended to guide Stage 2A research prioritisation, not to prescribe architecture or product scope.

| Theme | Key question | Evidence needed | Current confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence readiness | Are completed Stage 2A findings available elsewhere, and can important claims be traced to sources? | Source register, completed findings, unresolved-question log | High that repository evidence is incomplete; unknown beyond repository |
| Scope control | What is the smallest coherent customer, jurisdiction, asset and manufacturer boundary? | Design-partner evidence, workflow observations, source applicability | Low |
| Terminology and authority | What do inspection, serviceability, compliance, certification and recertification mean, and who may decide or issue each? | Practitioner interviews, operational documents, legal/domain review | Low |
| Source currency | Which laws, codes, standards, manufacturer instructions and client policies apply? | Authoritative source register with editions and effective dates | Low |
| Workflow validity | Does the provisional office/field/review workflow match real work and exceptions? | Contextual observation and anonymised job records | Low |
| Asset identity | How are missing, duplicate, replaced, untagged and newly discovered assets reconciled? | Real registers, labels, reports and field observation | Low |
| Offline operation | How long and how often are users disconnected, and which actions must remain available? | Site connectivity measurements and device tests | Low |
| Evidence integrity | What must be preserved to reproduce and defend an issued report? | Real reports, revision practices, contractual expectations | Low |
| Privacy and security | What personal, client-confidential and site-sensitive data is collected or cached? | Data map, privacy review, device and support workflows | Low |
| Retention | How long must each record class be kept, corrected, exported or deleted? | Legal, contractual and operational evidence | Low |
| Delivery feasibility | Can the provisional 120-day target be met within founder capacity and an explicit risk envelope? | Bounded scope, experiments, dependency map and forecast | Low |
| Commercial viability | Do onboarding, migration, support and evidence-maintenance costs fit likely pricing? | Design-partner data and measured cost model | Low |

## Candidate research order

1. Locate or complete the Stage 2A evidence package.
2. Bound the pilot customer, jurisdiction, assets and manufacturer coverage.
3. Establish terminology, actor authority and source applicability.
4. Observe real workflows and collect lawful, anonymised operational examples.
5. Measure connectivity, devices, data volumes and report expectations.
6. Only then define bounded technical experiments and acceptance measures.
