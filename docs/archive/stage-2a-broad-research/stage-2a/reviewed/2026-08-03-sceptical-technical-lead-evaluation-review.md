## Citation audit

The submitted citation markers are opaque conversation references rather than durable citations. A claim is not verified until the repository records at least:

- source title and issuing organisation;
- stable URL or local lawful source reference;
- publication or effective date;
- edition or version where applicable;
- relevant page, section or clause;
- access date;
- evidence class and applicability scope; and
- reviewer and confidence.

Paid or licensed standards must be stored only in `private-sources/` and must not be committed. Commit only lawful notes, metadata and short compliant extracts.

## Review disposition

- Preserve the evaluation as an unverified secondary source.
- Promote its major unknowns into the evidence-gap register.
- Promote only a small number of high-impact propositions into the assumptions register.
- Keep all stage criteria labelled `Proposed` until supported and approved.
- Do not record the reference architecture or recommended technical posture as a decision.
- Verify current Australian legal, regulator, standards and privacy claims from authoritative sources before use.
