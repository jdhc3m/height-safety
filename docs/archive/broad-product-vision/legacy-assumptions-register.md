# Legacy Stage 2A Assumptions Register

> Historical reference. This document does not define the current MVP scope.

This is the former register retained before the product was narrowed. Its
assumptions remain research history, not active requirements.

| ID | Former assumption | Status at archive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A-001 | A completed Stage 2A evidence package was not available in the repository. | Open |
| A-002 | A narrowly bounded pilot may be more feasible than national, multi-asset and multi-manufacturer coverage. | Open |
| A-003 | Inspection, compliance, certification and recertification may be used differently across actors or organisations. | Open |
| A-004 | Inspector, reviewer and certifier may be different people with different authority. | Open |
| A-005 | One generic checklist or outcome model may not safely cover all proposed asset types and manufacturers. | Open |
| A-006 | Representative field work may require offline capability, but the required extent was unknown. | Open |
| A-007 | Existing asset registers and identifiers may be incomplete, duplicated or inconsistent. | Open |
| A-008 | Issued reports may need reproducible source data, template and approval history. | Open |
| A-009 | A roughly 120-day pilot-ready target may be infeasible without tight scope. | Open |
