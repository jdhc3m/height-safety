# Stage Gates

Track stage-entry and stage-exit validation criteria here.

No gate below is approved. These are candidate governance criteria extracted from unverified secondary analysis and must be reviewed by the project owner after Stage 2A evidence is available.

## Proposed gate statuses

- Proposed — suggested but not approved.
- Ready for review — supporting evidence has been assembled.
- Approved — accepted by the named decision owner.
- Failed — evidence does not meet the approved criterion.
- Not applicable — excluded with a recorded reason.

## Candidate Stage 2B discovery exit gates

These criteria deliberately avoid selecting the final database, API, architecture, UX or MVP during Stage 2A.

| Gate ID | Proposed outcome | Evidence required | Decision owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G-EXT-01 | A narrow workflow is validated with practitioners. | Observations, exceptions, document corpus and participant review | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-02 | Every safety-significant pilot claim is traceable or explicitly excluded. | Applicability register and unresolved-conflict treatment | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-03 | Actor authority and report language are accepted for the pilot. | Qualified review and approved terminology/RACI | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-04 | Required offline behaviour is technically bounded and demonstrated without silent evidence loss. | Approved experiment design and results | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-05 | Issued-report integrity and revision expectations are demonstrated. | Approved provenance/reproduction experiment and results | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-06 | Privacy, security and tenant-boundary risks are acceptable for a controlled pilot. | Data map, threat review, test results and accepted residual risks | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-07 | Recovery and degraded-operation expectations are demonstrated. | Approved recovery objectives and exercise results | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-08 | Field usability is acceptable for the bounded workflow. | Approved task criteria and supervised field evidence | Unassigned | Proposed |
| G-EXT-09 | Commercial and delivery ranges are credible. | Unit-cost model, dependencies and range-based forecast with contingency | Project owner | Proposed |
| G-EXT-10 | The next decision preserves alternatives. | Documented recommendation: no-build, buy/integrate, narrow pilot or product engineering | Project owner | Proposed |

Numeric thresholds, participant counts and delivery periods must be justified and approved separately. They are not inherited from the submitted evaluation.
