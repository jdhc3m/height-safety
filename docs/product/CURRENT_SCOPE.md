# Current MVP Scope

## In scope

- New South Wales and Queensland sites, with jurisdiction explicitly recorded
  and one neutral evidence workflow.
- Read-only future-asset preview cards for industry conversations, such as
  static lines, guardrails and walkways; these are not inspectable assets.
- Company users with Administrator, Office reviewer and Inspector roles.
- Clients, sites, installed single-point roof-anchor register and basic history.
- Job preparation, assignment, Android field capture and office review.
- Limited offline work for one primary inspector on an assigned job.
- Notes, photographs, defects, recommendations, provisional outcomes and
  inaccessible/unable-to-locate/new-anchor exceptions.
- Visible synchronisation state, draft PDF generation and immutable revisions.

## Workflow boundary

Office creates client, site, anchors and job; releases it for an eligible
inspector to claim or assigns an inspector; inspector downloads, captures
evidence, submits; data synchronises; reviewer resolves
missing information and conflicts; connected office workflow generates a draft
report revision.

## Authority boundary

The system records human-entered evidence and organisational review. Final
approval remains connected and organisational. No automatic compliance,
certification, state-specific legal, engineering or standards conclusion is
produced. Jurisdiction does not automatically select intervals, licence rules,
proof-test rules or report authority. Future-asset previews do not create
register records, field forms, reports or compliance claims.
