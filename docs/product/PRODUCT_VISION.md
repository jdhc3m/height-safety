# Product Vision

An early-stage mobile and web application helps height-safety inspectors
prepare installed roof-anchor inspection jobs, identify anchors, record field
observations and photographs, review evidence, and generate draft inspection
reports.

Initial market: small Australian height-safety inspection companies in New
South Wales and Queensland. Initial asset: installed single-point roof anchors.
Delivery: Android field app, web office app, backend and relational database.
Pilot: one or two design partners able to validate both states.

The product supports workflow and professional judgement. It records submitted
observations and outcomes; it does not certify anchors, determine compliance,
interpret standards, issue engineer-approved certificates, or replace an
inspector, reviewer, engineer or certifier.

Preferred wording: draft inspection report; recorded inspection outcome;
submitted by inspector; reviewed by; report prepared by; further assessment
required; prototype workflow awaiting professional validation.
