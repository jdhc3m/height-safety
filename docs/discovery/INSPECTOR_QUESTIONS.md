# Inspector and Reviewer Questions

1. Walk through last complete inspection, in actual order.
2. Which steps happen office-side, onsite, after departure and during review?
3. What happens when an anchor is missing, inaccessible, damaged, unlabelled,
   duplicated, replaced or absent from the register?
4. Which checks, photographs, measurements, tools or decisions vary by product?
5. What do inspected, serviceable, defective, certified and further assessment
   required mean in your organisation?
6. Who may inspect, review, amend, approve wording or issue the document?
7. What evidence is needed six months later to explain an outcome or change?
8. Where and how long is connectivity lost? What must continue offline?
9. Which omissions and workarounds occur most often?
10. What would stop adoption after one job? What measurable improvement matters?

Capture exact words, examples, role/authority, frequency and evidence source.
Do not convert one participant's preference into a requirement without repeat
evidence.
