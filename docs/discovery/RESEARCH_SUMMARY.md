# Research Summary

## Evidence position

Repository research is broad Stage 2A background, not professional validation.
Archived material includes an unverified secondary technical-lead evaluation,
claim-verification notes, risk themes, evidence gaps and interview prompts.
Public claims retain source metadata and confidence where available; licensed
standards and private material are not committed.

## Current findings

- A narrow asset and jurisdiction boundary reduces workflow and authority risk.
- Asset identity, missing/unlocated/replaced/new anchors and report provenance
  are operational problems requiring explicit workflow.
- Field connectivity, photograph volume, device constraints and actual review
  practice remain unmeasured.
- Binary pass/fail, automatic intervals, universal manufacturer logic and
  standards-derived software rules are unsafe assumptions.
- Offline durability, idempotency, conflict visibility, tenant isolation and
  immutable report revisions are technical risks to prove early.

## Stage position

Stage 2A broad research is mostly complete and archived. Current work is Stage
2A.5 focused anchor evidence, followed by Stage 2B workflow specification.
Archive claims are leads, not requirements; promote facts only with durable
source details, applicability, confidence and review.
