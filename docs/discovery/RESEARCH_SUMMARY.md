# Research Summary

## Evidence position

Repository research is broad Stage 2A background plus a Stage 2A.5 public
focused-anchor pass and a New South Wales/Queensland scope extension. It is not
professional validation. Archived material
includes an unverified secondary technical-lead evaluation, claim-verification
notes, risk themes, evidence gaps and interview prompts. Public claims retain
source metadata and confidence where available; licensed standards and private
material are not committed.

## Current findings

- A narrow asset boundary and two explicit initial jurisdictions reduce
  workflow and authority risk while allowing a broader design-partner pool.
- Asset identity, missing/unlocated/replaced/new anchors and report provenance
  are operational problems requiring explicit workflow.
- Public sources support a durable anchor register, visual/condition evidence,
  photograph association, optional proof-test records, and neutral outcomes;
  they do not justify automatic compliance labelling.
- Public NSW and Queensland material supports a common neutral workflow for
  register identity, condition evidence, photographs, review and draft reports.
- State-specific interval, licensing, proof-test and approval-authority details
  are not sufficiently validated for automation; the product must record human
  inputs and sources rather than calculate statutory compliance.
- Field connectivity, photograph volume, device constraints and actual review
  practice remain unmeasured.
- Binary pass/fail, automatic intervals, universal manufacturer logic and
  standards-derived software rules are unsafe assumptions.
- Offline durability, idempotency, conflict visibility, tenant isolation and
  immutable report revisions are technical risks to prove early.
