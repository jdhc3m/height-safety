# Design-Partner Plan

Offer one company an early anchor-inspection workflow prototype, clearly not a
finished compliance product, in exchange for supervised workflow correction.

## Partner contribution

Named operational contact; interviews and demonstrations; supervised field
feedback; permissioned/anonymised registers, photographs, checklists, reports,
device and connectivity examples; weekly feedback during setup and use.

## Project contribution

Bounded Queensland single-point-anchor workflow; mobile capture; office review;
draft reports; onboarding and agreed support; transparent limitations. No
certificate, automatic compliance conclusion or replacement of existing process.

## Pilot controls

Limit access and purpose; private files by default; agree retention, export,
deletion and incident contacts before partner data. One primary inspector per
job, limited offline use, draft reports. Partner existing process remains
authoritative. Legal review required for data, branding and report terms.

## Success / exit

Success: representative job prepared, field visit completed without silent
loss, correct photo association after sync, reviewer finds missing information,
useful draft report, and repeatable corrections identified. Pause or exit if
permissions are unclear, evidence is lost, safe human review is impossible, or
feedback cannot be provided.
