# User Workflows

## Office preparation

1. Administrator or office reviewer signs in.
2. Reviewer creates a client and site.
3. Reviewer manually adds or imports installed anchors.
4. Reviewer creates a job, chooses the site and assigns one inspector.
5. The system presents the job pack and any missing register information.

## Inspector field capture

1. Inspector downloads the assigned job pack.
2. The app shows the site and existing anchor register, including identity and location.
3. Inspector opens each anchor, records observations, notes, photographs,
   defects, recommendations and one recorded outcome.
4. Inspector marks anchors inaccessible or unable to locate where applicable.
5. Inspector can add a newly discovered anchor and link evidence to it.
6. Local saves work without internet; sync and photograph states remain visible.
7. Inspector submits when required evidence is locally saved and the app
   records the submission intent.

## Office review and report

1. Submitted jobs appear in a review queue.
2. Reviewer sees missing information, failed uploads and important conflicts.
3. Reviewer requests changes or records review completion; reviewer does not
   silently overwrite safety-significant field evidence.
4. With connectivity, the system generates a neutral prototype draft PDF.
5. The generated revision is immutable as a file and points to its exact source
   inspection revision.
